Lisbon Macro Working Group

The Lisbon Macro Working Group brings together macroeconomists in Lisbon to present and discuss research at any stage where feedback is useful. We meet five times per year and presentations can range from preliminary ideas to papers being revised for journals. Presentations are one hour each.

Current members: João Brogueira de Sousa, Jesús Bueren, Marta Cota, Miguel H Ferreira, Nic Kozeniauskas, Francisco Queiró, Francisco Queirós, Ante Sterc, Tiago Tavares, Laszlo Tetenyi, and Edoardo Tolva.
